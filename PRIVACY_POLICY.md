# Privacy Policy — Taptico

**Effective date:** August 19, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 22, 2026  
**App:** Taptico (`com.orehruoy.taptico`)  
**Developer:** OrehRuoy  
**Hosted HTML:** [privacy.html](privacy.html) (GitHub Pages: `https://orehruoy.github.io/Taptico/privacy.html`)

## Summary

Taptico does not create accounts and does not show ads. Purchases stay with Apple. The app uses **Firebase / Google Analytics** to understand which fidgets people use. That data is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If you choose to send optional feedback from the in-app prompt, that note (and device/app version) is delivered to us through Web3Forms so we can improve the app. Email is included only if you type one and want a reply.

## Data We Collect

### On your device only

Motion sensors, haptics, and audio run locally. A small local file may remember Lifetime Unlock and whether we already asked if you are enjoying the app. That file is not uploaded to us.

### Analytics (Firebase / Google Analytics)

The app sends anonymous usage events, for example:

- Which fidget module is open
- When the Lifetime Unlock paywall is shown
- When you tap Unlock or Restore
- Whether the enjoy prompt was shown, and Yes / No / feedback sent (not the text of your note)

Google may process a device-level analytics identifier. We do **not** use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not track.

You can limit Apple’s advertising identifier in iOS Settings; Taptico does not use IDFA for ads.

### Optional feedback

After you have opened Taptico on three different days, we may ask if you are enjoying the app.

- **Yes** asks Apple to show the in-app review sheet. Apple decides whether it appears.
- **Not really** opens a form you can skip. If you send it, we receive your written note, optional email (only if you enter one), device model, OS version, screen size, and Taptico version. That helps us match bugs to a phone and build.

Feedback is sent with HTTPS to **Web3Forms**, which emails it to us. We do not use this for advertising. You can omit email. Closing the first question without answering means we may ask again on a later day.

## In-App Purchases

Lifetime Unlock is processed by **Apple StoreKit**. We do not receive payment details.

## Children's Privacy

Taptico is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Analytics events are not used to identify anyone. The feedback form is optional and does not require an email.
