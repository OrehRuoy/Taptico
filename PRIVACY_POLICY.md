## Summary

Taptico is a haptic fidget and focus tool for iPhone and Android. It does not create accounts, does not show ads, does not send analytics, and does not track you.

Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase through Apple on iPhone and Google Play on Android. We never see payment card details. Restore uses the store account (Apple ID or Google account).

If you choose to send optional feedback from the in-app prompt, that note is delivered to us through Web3Forms. The same form is used on both platforms.

## Data We Collect

### On your device only

Motion sensors, haptics, and audio run locally. A small local file may remember Lifetime Unlock, sound and haptics settings, how much of each fidget’s one-time free try is left, and whether we already asked if you are enjoying the app. That file is not uploaded to us.

iPhone haptics use Apple’s Taptic Engine / Core Haptics. Android haptics use the phone’s vibration motor.

### Optional feedback

After you have opened Taptico on three different days, we may ask if you are enjoying the app.

- On iPhone, **Yes** asks Apple to show the review sheet. On Android, **Yes** asks Google Play to show its in-app review sheet. Either store may decide not to show it.
- **Not really** opens a form you can skip. If you send it, we receive your message, device model, OS version, screen size, and Taptico version. Email is included only if you type one and want a reply.

Feedback is sent with HTTPS to **Web3Forms**, which emails it to us. We do not use this for advertising. You can omit email. Closing the first question without answering means we may ask again on a later day.

## In-App Purchases

Lifetime Unlock is processed by **Apple StoreKit** on iPhone and by **Google Play** on Android. We do not receive payment card details. Restore Purchases checks the store account, not a server we operate.

## Deleting your data

Email [hallanhype@gmail.com](mailto:hallanhype@gmail.com) to ask us to delete any feedback you sent, and the email address you gave with it. We will delete it within 30 days.

Local app data (the purchase flag and free-try timers) is removed by deleting the app, or by clearing the app’s storage on Android. Purchase records remain with Apple or Google Play.

## Android permissions

The Android app requests vibration (haptics) and internet (Google Play purchases and optional feedback only). It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or the advertising ID.

## Children's Privacy

Taptico’s App Store rating is 4+. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The feedback form is optional and does not require an email.
