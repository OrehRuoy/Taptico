Taptico: Haptic Fidget Toys

Desk slider, clicker & lock.

Taptico is a quiet set of metallic fidget toys, with real clicks, pops, and slides you can feel on your phone. No ads. No account. Three toys free forever.

On iPhone, the clicks come from the Taptic Engine. On Android, they come from the phone's vibration motor. Every toy is a small gold-and-steel object you can actually feel.

Free forever

  • Combination Lock — spin the dial and feel every detent
  • Toggle Switches — heavy industrial snaps
  • Magnetic Slider — drag until it snaps to either end

Try before you buy

Every locked toy has a one-time 5-minute free try, so you can feel it before you decide.

Lifetime unlock

One purchase, priced for your region. No subscription. It opens every toy and Desk Stand mode:

  • Desk Cube — analog stick, gear, worry stone, clicker dots
  • Infinity Cube — fold eight cubes that stay in one piece
  • Clicker Pen — a retractable click with a writing tip
  • Metal Spinner, Zipper, and Dimple pop-it plate
  • Bearing Chamber, Key Matrix, and Ambient Pad

Desk Stand mode: stand your phone on its side while it charges, and Taptico turns landscape and keeps the screen on.

Make it yours

  • Haptics, volume, and shake/tilt strength sliders
  • Sound only (headphones), haptics only, or both
  • Left-hand, right-hand, or two-hand layout

Restore Purchases is always on the paywall. Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Traditional Chinese. Requires iOS 16 or later on iPhone and iPad; also on Google Play for Android.
