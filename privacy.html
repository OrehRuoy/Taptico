Taptico

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026
App: Taptico · iOS bundle ID: com.orehruoy.taptico · Android package: com.taptico.fidgettoys · Developer: OrehRuoy

Taptico does not create accounts, does not show ads, and does not send analytics or track you. Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase through Apple on iPhone and Google Play on Android. We never see payment card details. Optional in-app feedback is sent to us only if you choose to submit it. On iPhone, an optional crash report is included only if you check that box.

1. Who we are

Taptico is a haptic fidget and focus tool for iPhone and Android, published by OrehRuoy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share:

  • Name, email, or other contact details (except if you email us, or optionally type an email on the in-app feedback form)
  • Advertising identifiers used for ads, or tracking pixels
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Health or fitness data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

Motion, haptics, and audio stay on the device. Taptico does not send usage events to an analytics service.

3. Optional feedback

After you open Taptico on three different calendar days, we may ask if you are enjoying the app. On iPhone, Yes asks Apple to show the review sheet. On Android, Yes asks Google Play to show its in-app review sheet. Either store may decide not to show it. Not really opens a form. If you send it, we receive your message and, with it, device model, OS version, screen size, and app version so we can match a problem to a phone and build. Email is included only if you type one and want a reply. On iPhone, an optional, user-chosen crash report can be attached to that feedback. The box is unchecked unless you select it. The report stays on the device until you attach it, and that file is deleted after it is sent. That message is delivered over HTTPS by Web3Forms and then emailed to us. We do not use it for advertising or analytics. This form works the same on iPhone and Android.

4. What stays on your device

The app may keep a small local file on the device so it can remember whether you purchased Lifetime Unlock, how much of each fidget’s one-time free try is left, and whether we already asked if you are enjoying the app. That file never leaves the device and is not uploaded to us. On iPhone, a crash or hang report may also be saved on the device. It is not uploaded unless you choose to attach it to feedback.

Motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) are used only on-device to drive fidget physics and lighting. Sensor readings are not recorded or transmitted.

Haptic feedback stays on the device. iPhone uses Apple’s Taptic Engine / Core Haptics. Android uses the phone’s vibration motor.

5. In-app purchases

Lifetime Unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase. On iPhone it is processed by Apple through StoreKit. On Android it is processed by Google Play. The store handles payment and the receipt. We never see your payment card details, and we do not receive your Apple ID or Google account password, or a copy of your billing address.

Restore Purchases asks the store whether this store account already owns Lifetime Unlock. On iPhone that account is the Apple ID. On Android it is the Google account. The check is performed by Apple or Google, not by a server we operate.

6. Deleting your data

Email hallanhype@gmail.com to ask us to delete any feedback you sent, and the email address you gave with it. We will delete it within 30 days.

Local app data, including the purchase flag and free-try timers, stays on the device. Deleting the app removes it. On Android, clearing the app’s storage removes it as well. Purchase records remain with Apple or Google Play. After you reinstall, Restore Purchases checks the same store account.

7. Android permissions

The Android app requests vibration, for haptics, and internet, for Google Play purchases and for optional feedback only. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or the advertising ID.

8. Children’s privacy

Taptico’s App Store rating is 4+. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The feedback form is optional and does not require an email.

9. Tracking

Taptico does not track you across apps or websites for advertising, on iPhone or Android. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. On iPhone we do not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not use data for tracking. The iOS privacy manifest sets tracking to false and does not declare analytics collection. The Android app does not use the advertising ID.

10. International users

Optional feedback may be processed by Web3Forms outside your country. Purchases are delivered by Apple or Google Play in your region.

11. Your choices

You can ignore the enjoy prompt. You can skip the feedback form, leave the email field blank, and leave the crash-report box unchecked. To delete feedback you already sent, or to remove local app data, see Deleting your data above.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated date will change at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store and Google Play listings where required.

13. Contact
