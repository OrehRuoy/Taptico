Taptico

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026 · Last updated: August 20, 2026
App: Taptico · Bundle ID: com.orehruoy.taptico · Developer: OrehRuoy

Taptico does not create accounts and does not show ads. Purchases stay with Apple. The app uses Firebase / Google Analytics for anonymous usage (which fidgets people open, paywall taps). That is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Taptico is a haptic fidget and focus tool for iPhone, published by OrehRuoy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share:

  • Name, email, or other contact details (except if you email us yourself)
  • Advertising identifiers used for ads, or tracking pixels
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Health, fitness, or diagnostic data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

The app sends anonymous usage events to Firebase / Google Analytics (for example which fidget is open, and whether Lifetime Unlock is shown or purchased). Google may process a device-level analytics identifier. We do not use this to identify you, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

3. What stays on your device

The app may keep a small local file on the device so it can remember whether you purchased Lifetime Unlock. That file never leaves the device and is not uploaded to us.

Motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) are used only on-device to drive fidget physics and lighting. Sensor readings are not recorded or transmitted.

Haptic feedback uses Apple’s Taptic Engine / Core Haptics on-device.

4. In-app purchases

Lifetime Unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment, Apple ID, and receipts. We do not receive your card number, Apple ID password, or a copy of your billing address.

Restore Purchases asks Apple whether this Apple ID already owns Lifetime Unlock. That check is performed by Apple’s systems, not by a server we operate.

5. Children’s privacy

Taptico is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Tracking

Taptico does not track you across apps or websites for advertising. We do not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not use data for tracking. The app’s privacy manifest sets tracking to false. Analytics is declared as product interaction / device ID for analytics only.

7. International users

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not transfer personal data to other countries. The app’s content and purchases are delivered by Apple in your region.

8. Your choices

You can delete the app at any time, which removes local entitlement data stored by Taptico on that device. Purchase records remain with Apple; use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

If you emailed us, you may ask us to delete that email thread.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated date will change at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store listing where required.
